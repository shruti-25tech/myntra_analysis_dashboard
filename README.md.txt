# 🛍️ Myntra Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Myntra Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance, customer orders, product distribution, and brand performance. It transforms sales data into meaningful visual insights to help understand business trends and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze monthly sales and discount trends.
- Compare sales performance across brands and product categories.
- Understand order distribution across customer segments.
- Identify weekday sales patterns.
- Visualize state-wise and regional order distribution.
- Provide interactive filtering for detailed analysis.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- **KPI Cards:** Key metrics including product count, average ratings, average discount, total sales, and total orders.
- **Sales and Discount Trend:** Monthly comparison of sales and average discounts.
- **Category Analysis:** Compare sales performance across categories.
- **Weekday Analysis:** Understand sales patterns across different days.
- **Brand Distribution:** Analyze orders and sales across brands.
- **Product Distribution:** Explore product-level distribution.
- **Color and Size Analysis:** Understand product variations.
- **Regional Analysis:** Visualize orders by state and region.
- **Interactive Filters:** Filter data by brand, category, sub-category, product, size, and year.
